Processes, Profits & People

The Complete Guide to Business Process and Operations

The book reflects Wilfred’s professional interest in the relationship between people, processes, systems, operations, and profitability. Wilfred’s experience as both a professional and entrepreneur gives the subject a practical dimension — grounded in working within established organizations and in building and managing businesses across different sectors.
